Frequently asked questions

What is DCA backtesting?
Backtesting replays a strategy on real historical prices. Here it simulates buying a fixed dollar amount of a coin on a regular schedule, so you can see what that habit would be worth today instead of guessing.
Where does the price data come from?
Weekly closing prices are loaded live in your browser from public exchange market data (Binance Vision). Nothing is stored on a server and there is no sign-up. History typically starts around 2017 for Bitcoin and Ethereum and later for newer coins.
Does a good backtest mean future profit?
No. Crypto has had large bull runs in its history, which flatters past results. Backtests show what happened, not what will happen. Never invest more than you can afford to lose.
