Frequently asked questions

How does leverage affect profit and loss?
Leverage multiplies your exposure. At 10× leverage, a 1% move in price becomes roughly a 10% change in your margin — in both directions. Higher leverage means bigger gains and bigger losses.
What is return on margin?
It is your profit or loss divided by the margin you put up, not the full position value. This is the number that reflects what actually happened to your own money.
How much can the price move before liquidation?
Roughly 100 ÷ leverage percent against you (a little less after maintenance margin and fees). At 10× that is about a 10% adverse move; at 50× only about 2%.
