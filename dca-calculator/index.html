Frequently asked questions

What is dollar-cost averaging (DCA)?
DCA means buying a fixed dollar amount at regular intervals regardless of price. It smooths out your entry over time, so you buy more coins when prices are low and fewer when high, removing the pressure of timing the market.
How is my average price calculated?
Average price = total dollars invested ÷ total coins bought. Each buy adds its dollars to the total and amount÷price coins to your stack; the calculator divides the two.
Does DCA guarantee a profit?
No. DCA reduces timing risk but you still profit only if the asset's price ends up above your average entry. It is a discipline for managing volatility, not a guarantee of returns.
