Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate percentage change between two prices?
Percentage change = (final − initial) ÷ initial × 100. A positive result is a gain, a negative result is a loss.
Why does a 50% loss need a 100% gain to recover?
Because the gain is measured from the smaller, post-loss amount. Halving $100 leaves $50, and going from $50 back to $100 is a 100% gain. The deeper the loss, the more lopsided this gets.
What is the multiplier?
It is the final price divided by the initial price — how many times your money you ended with. 2× means it doubled; 0.5× means it halved.
