Frequently asked questions

What does 'averaging down' mean?
Averaging down is buying more of an asset after its price has fallen, which lowers your average entry price. Your break-even price drops, so a smaller recovery returns you to profit.
Is averaging down a good idea?
It lowers your break-even but also increases your exposure to a losing position. It works when the asset recovers, and hurts when it keeps falling. Size new buys against your risk plan.
How is the new average calculated?
New average = (old coins × old average + new dollars) ÷ (old coins + new coins). The calculator does this for you and shows how much your average moves.
