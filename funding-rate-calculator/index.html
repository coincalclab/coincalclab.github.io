Most exchanges charge funding every 8 hours (3× per day). When the rate is positive, longs pay shorts; when negative, shorts pay longs. This shows the amount you pay (or receive).

Frequently asked questions

What is a funding rate?
Funding is a periodic payment between long and short traders on perpetual futures that keeps the contract price tied to the spot price. It is not a fee to the exchange — it flows between traders.
How often is funding charged?
On most exchanges every 8 hours, so three times a day. The quoted rate is usually per 8-hour interval, which is why small-looking rates add up over days of holding.
Can funding ever pay me?
Yes. If you hold the side that receives funding (for example a short when the rate is positive), you are paid instead of charged. This tool shows the magnitude either way.
