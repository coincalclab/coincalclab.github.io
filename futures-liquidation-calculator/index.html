Frequently asked questions

How is the liquidation price calculated?
For an isolated long it is approximately entry × (1 − 1/leverage + maintenance margin rate). For a short it is entry × (1 + 1/leverage − maintenance margin rate). Higher leverage moves the liquidation price closer to your entry.
Why does my exchange show a slightly different price?
Exchanges apply tiered maintenance margin, funding payments and fees, and some use cross margin. This calculator gives a clean isolated-margin estimate, which is usually within a fraction of a percent of the real trigger.
How can I avoid liquidation?
Use lower leverage, add margin, or set a stop-loss well before the liquidation price. The lower your leverage, the larger the adverse move you can survive.
