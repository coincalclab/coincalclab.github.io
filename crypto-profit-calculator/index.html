Frequently asked questions

How is crypto profit calculated?
Profit = (net proceeds from selling) − (amount invested). Net proceeds are your coins multiplied by the sell price, minus the selling fee. The amount invested already includes your buying fee, so the calculator nets both fees out for you.
What is the break-even price?
The break-even sell price is the price at which your proceeds exactly equal what you put in, after both fees. Selling above it is a profit; below it is a loss.
Does this include trading fees?
Yes. Enter your exchange's fee per side (for example 0.1%). The tool charges it on both the buy and the sell, which is what actually happens on most exchanges.
