Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between APR and APY?
APR is the simple annual rate. APY includes the effect of compounding — earning interest on your interest — so for the same APR, more frequent compounding gives a higher APY.
How does compounding frequency change my returns?
More frequent compounding (daily vs yearly) increases the final balance because rewards start earning their own rewards sooner. The effect grows over longer time horizons.
Are crypto staking yields safe?
No yield is risk-free. Staking rewards can change, tokens can be locked for a period, and the token's price can fall further than the yield you earn. Always weigh yield against price risk.
