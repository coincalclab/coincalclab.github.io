Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate a take-profit price?
For a long, take-profit price = entry × (1 + required price move). The required price move equals your target return divided by leverage, because leverage multiplies a price move into your return.
Why does leverage lower the price I need?
At 10× leverage a 5% price move produces a 50% return on margin. So to hit a given return you need a smaller move — but the same leverage magnifies losses just as fast in the other direction.
Does this include fees?
No — it shows the raw target. Trading fees and funding slightly raise the price you actually need, so set your target a little higher to cover them.
