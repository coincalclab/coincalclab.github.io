Frequently asked questions

What is a good risk/reward ratio?
Many traders aim for at least 1:2 — risking one to make two. The higher the reward relative to risk, the lower the win rate you need to stay profitable, which gives you more room for error.
How is the break-even win rate calculated?
Break-even win rate = risk ÷ (risk + reward). At 1:1 you need to win 50% of the time; at 1:3 only 25%. It tells you the minimum accuracy required before fees to avoid losing money.
Does a high ratio guarantee profit?
No. A great ratio is worthless if your targets are unrealistic and rarely hit. Ratio and win rate must be judged together, along with fees and slippage.
